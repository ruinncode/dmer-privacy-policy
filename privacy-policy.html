dmer Privacy Policy

Distraction-free Instagram DM Client

Summary: dmer does not collect, store, or transmit any user data. Your privacy is fully protected.

What Data We Collect

None dmer does not collect any personal information, browsing data, or usage statistics.

What the Extension Does

dmer is a browser extension that modifies the visual appearance of Instagram to help you focus on direct messages. It works by:

  • Injecting CSS styles to hide distracting elements (feed, stories, reels, explore)
  • Running a small JavaScript to block navigation to distracting pages
  • Storing your on/off preference locally on your device

What We Do NOT Do

Activity Status
Collect personal information No
Read your messages or DMs No
Access your login credentials No
Send data to external servers No
Track your browsing activity No
Use cookies or tracking pixels No
Access other websites No
Modify your posts or messages No

Permissions Explained

storage: Used solely to save your preference for enabling or disabling the extension. This data never leaves your device.

host_permissions (instagram.com): Required to inject the CSS that hides distracting elements on Instagram. The extension only operates on instagram.com and no other websites.

Data Storage

The only data stored is your extension preference (on/off toggle), which is saved locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Is deleted when you uninstall the extension

Third Parties

dmer does not share any information with third parties because we do not collect any information in the first place.

Open Source

dmer is open source. You can review all the code to verify our privacy practices.
